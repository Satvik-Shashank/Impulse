Model Performance & Cost-Sensitive Decision Curves

Evaluated on the held-out test split (750 disputes) never seen during training.

Cost vs. Threshold Curve Cost-Optimal Gate: 0.50
Curve Guide & Financial Optimization
  • Net ₹ / Dispute (Green Curve): The average rupee value gained per dispute after deducting filing fees and manual review costs. Peaks at 0.50 threshold (₹1,258/dispute).
  • Auto-Respond % (Purple Line): Percentage of disputes auto-submitted without human review. At 0.50, ~6.5% of clean, verified cases are resolved instantly.
  • Win Rate % (Orange Line): Empirical win rate of cases submitted at this gate (~85% win rate when evidence package is complete).
  • Takeaway: Setting higher thresholds (0.95) acts too conservatively and forfeits recoverable funds. The 0.50 gate maximizes net rupee recovery.
Feature Importances (Top Signals) LightGBM Gini Score
Feature Signals & Decision Drivers
  • Customer Account Age (Top Signal): Long-standing accounts rarely file first-party fraud compared to newly registered throwaway accounts.
  • Days to Dispute: Chargebacks filed within 7 days strongly indicate unauthorized card theft, whereas 45+ days indicate merchandise or return issues.
  • Dispute Amount & Category: High-ticket Electronics and Luxury items have distinct fraud signatures compared to low-ticket Grocery orders.
  • 3DS & Delivery Proof: Direct cryptographic authentication and courier signatures provide the strongest deterministic defense in representment.

Security & Compliance

          
Defense-Only Guardrails & Documented Failures

          
Architectural constraints that guarantee security, zero PII exposure, and deterministic fallbacks.

        


        

          
Operational Guardrails

          

            

              
GuardrailImplementation Detail

              
                
No Dispute GenerationSystem only responds defensively to incoming chargeback webhooks; it never initiates or manufactures disputes.

                
No PII Exposure100% synthetic data generated via Faker (Indian BFSI context). No real customer data is stored or logged.

                
No Raw Card DataCard numbers are masked/tokenized upstream. System only accepts boolean match signals (avs_cvv_match).

                
Auditable Evidence RulesReason-code-to-evidence mapping is a deterministic rule table derived from published Visa/Mastercard representment specs.

                
Emergency Kill SwitchSet AUTO_RESPOND_CONFIDENCE = 1.0 to instantly route 100% of disputes to human review.

              
            

          

        


        

          
Documented Failure Modes & Handling

          

            

              
Failure ModeSystem BehaviorWhy This Is the Right Behavior

              
                

                  Weak / Absent Evidence
                  HUMAN_REVIEW — never auto-submitted
                  Auto-submit requires both confidence ≥ 70% and evidence strength ≥ 60% with ≥ 2 compelling pieces.
                

                

                  Ambiguous Mixed-Signal
                  Platt calibration keeps confidence low → routes to review
                  Platt scaling ensures confidence represents true probability, not an uncalibrated heuristic score.
                

                

                  Unknown Reason Code
                  Falls back to generic.j2 with auto_respond_eligible = false
                  System fails closed, not open, on unrecognized inputs.
                

                

                  High-Value + Strong Evidence
                  Subject to standard threshold logic (no special-casing)
                  Prevents arbitrary human overrides when stakes are highest — the same rules apply regardless of amount.
